Measures

DV = mean of 0–100 confidence ratings for texture, gloss, shape and stitching. Colour is collected but excluded from the DV. Scope inference uses two 1–7 items. Manipulation checks are asked last.

Analysis

The dashboard demonstrates the planned 2 × 2 factorial ANOVA: main effect of disclosure specificity (H1), main effect of accountability (H2), and their interaction (H3). It also shows the pre-specified D-versus-A contrast, reliability, scope-inference process evidence, and manipulation-check accuracy.

Backend

Double-click RUN_BACKEND_WINDOWS.bat to run the local zero-dependency Python server. It serves the site at http://127.0.0.1:8000 and saves live survey submissions to SQLite. Without the backend, the site still works in static mode and survey submissions download locally as CSV.
